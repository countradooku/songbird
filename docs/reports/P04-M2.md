## What was built

`songbird_content::state` is the reference every app reproduces: pure, no I/O.

1. **`lww`**: last write per author by the author's counter. Built on it:
   - **reactions** (add, replace, remove);
   - **RSVPs**.
2. **`poll`**:
   - each voter's highest counter counts, and invalid votes count as none;
   - a close by the poll's author fixes exactly the votes it lists, and other closes are
     dropped;
   - votes may arrive before the poll; at most 64 votes are kept per voter.
3. **`edits`**: the author's edits with the highest revision, and history; delete for everyone.
   Both use the 24-hour window, judged from the messages' own times.
4. **`chat`**: pins (duration, at most 3, the newest winning), the disappearing timer and the
   restricted-chat policy, by the greatest (time, author, message ID).
5. **`status`**:
   - sending, sent, delivered, read, played and failed, each recipient's greatest receipt
     winning;
   - group "Message info" with the audience fixed at send time;
   - the reciprocal read-receipt setting, applied to groups (D-0058).
6. **`receipts`** (the coalescer):
   - one receipt per (chat, author, kind) per window;
   - "read up to here" for reads, at most 512 IDs per receipt;
   - one `ReadSync` to the reader's own devices;
   - the window is 1 s, or 10 s in groups above 32 members.
7. Docs:
   - PROTOCOL.md §5.5 and §5.6;
   - threat model §3.18;
   - D-0060.

## The receipt load test

`tests/e2e/tests/receipts_load.rs`: the real coalescer on every device drives the real send
path (`SendPath` over the in-process mailbox). Every envelope is sealed and padded content, and
every envelope counted was stored.

- **Setup:** 1,024 members with 2 devices each; 60 messages in one hour; 80 % of devices online,
  the rest catching up at a random time; 3 chat openings per member.
- **Run:** Apple M5 Pro, release; the 8 scenarios took 1.5 s.

| Traffic | Window | Delivered from | Envelopes | Receipts | Share of envelopes | Share of bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steady (random authors) | 1 s | every device | 458,794 | 333,268 | 72.6 % | 70.9 % |
| Steady | 1 s | primaries | 336,000 | 210,474 | 62.6 % | 60.6 % |
| Steady | 10 s | every device | 458,788 | 333,262 | 72.6 % | 70.9 % |
| Steady | 10 s | primaries | 335,996 | 210,470 | 62.6 % | 60.6 % |
| Bursts of two people chatting | 1 s | every device | 367,209 | 242,064 | 65.9 % | 63.8 % |
| Bursts | 1 s | primaries | 255,527 | 130,382 | 51.0 % | 48.7 % |
| **Bursts** | **10 s** | **every device** | **236,934** | **111,789** | **47.2 %** | **44.9 %** |
| Bursts | 10 s | primaries | 190,589 | 65,444 | 34.3 % | 32.3 % |

Group messages are 122,820 envelopes in every case: 60 messages to 2,047 devices each.

What the numbers say:

- **Receipts go to each author only** (the prompt's rule), so they batch only across one
  author's messages. With steady traffic from random authors, no window helps.
- **When people chat in bursts**, a 10-second window removes more than half the receipts. Hence
  the protocol's 10 s above 32 members.
- **"Read up to here"** saves little in a big group: one opening reads messages from many
  authors (9,624 read receipts for 47,360 messages read). In a 1:1 chat it is one receipt
  (`opening_a_chat_with_200…`).
- **Delivered receipts from primaries only** would save another 13 points, but "delivered" would
  then wait for each member's primary. That is a product choice, listed for the owner below.

## Open risks

- Receipts remain close to half the envelopes of a busy large group: at 2,048 devices that is
  about 1,900 extra envelopes per message. Small (160 bytes padded), but each costs a mailbox
  entry and an acknowledgement. Prompt 5's group tiers can lower it further.
- Pins, timers and policies trust the sender's clock where a message carries no server stamp:
  devices still agree, but a skewed clock decides who wins.

## For the owner

No threat-model §1 row: the server sees nothing new. One choice is offered with the Prompt 4
gate: whether delivered receipts in large groups (above 32 members) come from primaries only
(34 % instead of 47 % in the busy case above). Recommended: keep every device for now, and
decide with Prompt 5's group tiers.
