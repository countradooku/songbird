## The server's view of every setting

THREAT_MODEL §4 now lists each server-enforced setting, who changes it and where it is enforced;
every other setting is an encrypted record (M1) or inside the encrypted profile.

## Changes from the plan

- The server's default for group adds is "everyone"; the app sets "contacts" at registration
  (so existing accounts and tests keep working).
- Discoverability off keeps the account's own username links working through a `link` field on
  lookups (the plan said links keep working; this is how).
