## The simulations (the prompt's gate)

| Simulation | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Registration flood** from one range: 31 sign-ups in an hour | Work doubled at 3, 10 and 30 (and once more when the deployment passed its step), capped at 16× the base; a person in another range paid only the deployment's step (2×) | `registration_live::a_registration_flood_raises_the_work_for_its_range_and_barely_for_others` |
| **Request spam** from a new account to 24 strangers | Its first ten went through (as an established user's did); the eleventh waited more than three times longer than an established account's; after the fifth block by people it messaged it was limited (two requests, then an hour's wait), its contacts unaffected; ten strangers blocking an established user it never messaged changed nothing | `gate_p11::request_spam_is_slowed_then_limited_and_normal_users_are_not` |
| **Report brigade**: 200 accounts against one person | 199 without a message from the target were refused; the one recipient's report counted once (a second: duplicate); the target was limited only at its fifth distinct reported direct message | `gate_p11::a_report_brigade_changes_nothing_without_messages` |
| **Suspension** | Sends refused on the WebSocket and over HTTP (15 `Suspended`); no sender certificate; it still received, exported and deleted itself; an automatic limit did not override it; lifted, it sent again | `gate_p11::a_suspended_account_receives_exports_deletes_but_cannot_send` |
| Normal users | Prompt 4's gate unchanged but for its flood test, which now sets the new-account budget it measures | `gate_p04` (5 of 5) |

## Changes from the plan

- **Signals are evaluated when they arrive**, by the request that knows the account (a report,
  a block), instead of a periodic job: counters stay under keyed tags no job could invert.
- **Only direct messages** count towards an automatic limit; group reports go to moderators
  only (a group of brigaders could otherwise do it).
- **Only blocks by people the account sent a request to** count (a strangers' brigade cannot).
- **No proof of work on each request** for limited accounts: a budget of one an hour (burst
  two) does the same with nothing for the client to change.
- New-account age is read from the registry at each request (requests are rare), so a
  brand-new account is never mistaken for an old one between snapshot reloads.
- A suspended account's sealed sends stop when its current certificate expires (at most
  `certificate_hours`), since the server cannot see a sealed sender.
