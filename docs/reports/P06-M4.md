## Owner decisions in this milestone

Threat model §1, two rows awaiting approval:

- pack records (no account once complete; the creating device for at most 24 h before that);
- GIF queries in `api`'s memory, sent to GIPHY with nothing of the app.

Before production, the owner provides:

- GIPHY's API key, into SOPS;
- acceptance of GIPHY's terms, which cover caching and proxying their files.

## Open risks

- GIPHY's terms may require attribution in the app ("Powered by GIPHY"). That is a UI matter for
  the apps.
- The GIF caches are per node. Many nodes multiply provider traffic up to the cluster budget,
  which bounds it.
