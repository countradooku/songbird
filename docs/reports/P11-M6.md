## Changes from the plan

- The **bearer and ticket ID are drawn by the server** and returned once (not chosen by the
  client): the server cannot otherwise vouch for their entropy, and they are stored hashed.
- Signing a ticket is how the user **attaches** their account; the app signs only when the user
  says the ticket is about their account (an appeal, a billing question).
- The support budget's key is derived from the push index secret every `api` node already has,
  instead of a new secret to provision (HKDF with its own label).
- The invite link is a single base64url blob (130 characters with a code) rather than dotted
  hex fields: shorter to share, versioned.
