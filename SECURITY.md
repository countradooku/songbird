# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:
open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We will acknowledge the
report, keep you informed while we fix it, and credit you if you wish.

## Scope

The Songbird backend services and shared crates in this repository. A bug-bounty policy and
safe-harbour text will be published before the public beta (Prompt 12).

## Our commitments

- Every vulnerability found becomes a permanent regression test.
- Fixes are released before details are disclosed.
