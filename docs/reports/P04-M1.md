## What was built

1. **`crates/content/proto/content.proto`**, the specification apps generate their types from.
   It defines:
   - the layers (padded `Plaintext`, then `Content`) and the rules every version keeps;
   - every bound next to its field.
2. **The Rust types** (`schema.rs`), field for field, with `Debug` printing type names only.
3. **What apps call** (`lib.rs`):
   - `seal` encodes, frames, pads and returns the franking commitment;
   - `open` unpads, decodes and validates, with sizes checked before decoding;
   - `Opened::franking_matches` checks the commitment the server stamped;
   - `needs_update` reports a message this app is too old to show.
4. **Validation** (`validate.rs`): every bound in the specification. Unknown fields, kinds and
   enum values are accepted, and nesting stops at one level.
5. **What the schema covers:**
   - **chat messages:**
     - text with formatting spans and mentions;
     - replies, reactions (add, replace, remove), edits with revisions, delete for everyone, and
       the forward counter;
     - voice notes and attachment pointers (Prompt 6 extends them), contact cards, location
       pins, sticker references and link previews;
     - polls (create, vote, close), events and RSVPs, pins with a duration, timer changes,
       view-once, nudges, call events, group and channel system messages, and the
       restricted-chat policy;
   - **receipts** (delivered, read, played; batched, with "up to here") and **typing**;
   - **message-request responses** and **profile-key sharing**;
   - **sync messages:** sent transcripts (including broadcast lists), read, viewed, starred, chat
     state (archived, muted, pinned, unread), settings, the block list, call history and request
     responses.
6. **Vectors and the compatibility suite:**
   - `vectors/v1/vectors.json` holds 33 vectors;
   - `tests/compat.rs` decodes every version directory with this reader, plus a simulated newer
     writer's fields at three depths;
   - `tools/content-vectors` (outside the workspace) checks the vectors with an independent
     protobuf compiler.
7. **Docs:**
   - PROTOCOL.md §5.1–5.4 and §5.7–5.12: layers, versions, franking on the client, chat messages,
     typing, sync, restricted chat, and the client-only features;
   - §12 (compatibility rules) and §13 (vectors);
   - threat model §3.17; D-0059; fuzz target `content`, in the nightly matrix.

## Open risks

- Apps must keep their generated types in step with `content.proto`. The vectors and the
  compatibility suite are their conformance tests.
- Restricted chats and forwarding limits are enforced by honest apps only (PROTOCOL §5.4, §5.9).
- Edit and delete windows rely on the server's stamp time where franked (M3), otherwise on the
  sender's clock.
