## Benchmarks

**Machine:** AMD Ryzen 9 7950X (16 cores, 32 threads), 31 GiB; Windows 11 Pro 10.0.26300;
`rustc 1.99.1`, release profile.

**Method:**

- **Clients:** OpenMLS 0.9.2 on its RustCrypto provider (the harness).
- **Delivery service:** `crates/groups` on Songbird's verify-only aws-lc-rs provider. For each
  commit it does what `api` does: load the stored record, validate and merge, store the new
  record.
- **Filling the group:** the owner adds everyone in batches of 256, then the measured operations
  run on the full group:
  - an add (commit and Welcome), and the newcomer joining from the Welcome and the tree;
  - a member's key update;
  - the owner removing a member.
- **Classical rows** have client and wire numbers only. The delivery service refuses classical
  groups (D-0065 §3).

Run it:

```
cargo test --release -p songbird-groups-harness --test scale -- --ignored --nocapture
```

**Batched adds leave the tree's parent nodes blank, so this is the worst case for a full-path
commit** (update, remove): its path secrets are encrypted to nearly every leaf. The best case
is a full tree, where a path is one encryption per level. That case is computed, not measured:

| Members | X-Wing, full tree | Classical, full tree |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,024 | about 24 KiB | about 1.1 KiB |
| 2,048 | about 26 KiB | about 1.2 KiB |
| 5,000 | about 31 KiB | about 1.4 KiB |

Each level carries an HPKE key and one ciphertext: 1,216 + 1,120 + 48 bytes for X-Wing, 32 + 32
+ 48 bytes for classical. A real group lies in between, and moves toward the full tree as
members update.

### What the numbers decide

- **Time is not the limit.**
  - The delivery service validates a commit in 13 ms at 1,024 X-Wing members and 71 ms at
    5,000, on one core.
  - The whole group's state is loaded and stored each time: about 2 ms per MiB to load and 1
    to store.
  - Clients take at most a third of a second, even joining a group of 5,000.
- **Bytes are.**
  - At 5,000 X-Wing leaves a full-path commit of a fresh tree is 5.6 MiB, over the 4 MiB bundle
    limit, and every member would download it.
  - At 2,048 it is 2.3 MiB, inside the limit.
  - The stored state, rewritten by every commit, is 1.3 MiB at 1,024 and 2.7 MiB at 2,048.
- **Group limit:** 1,024 leaves (devices), as planned.
- **Private-channel cap:** 2,048 leaves, the largest power of two whose worst-case commit fits
  the bundle limit.
- **X-Wing stays the default** (D-0065 §3 asked for these numbers):
  - Welcomes (1.4 KiB) and add commits (2.8 KiB) are small at every size.
  - X-Wing's cost is in full-path commits: 14 times classical on a fresh tree (1.2 MiB against
    83 KiB at 1,024), about 24 KiB once the tree fills.
  - Classical would leave every group's history open to "harvest now, decrypt later".
  - **For the owner:** confirm X-Wing with this cost, or ask for classical in large groups.
- **Apps should commit a key update soon after joining a large group** (it fills their path
  for everyone's later commits). That is in the apps' guidance, not enforced.
- **rayon:**
  - OpenMLS uses it only to encrypt a new path, in `treekem.rs` and `parent_node.rs`. Only a
    committing client does that.
  - The delivery service validates and merges; it never builds a path, so `api` starts no
    rayon threads.
  - Nothing to configure.
