## The evaluation

`akd` 0.13.0 (Meta, MIT/Apache-2.0, used by WhatsApp) adds 12 crates to the tree, including a
second Ed25519 and SHA-2 stack (`ed25519-dalek`, `sha2`) and `protobuf`. Measured with
`cargo tree` against the workspace. Its real cost is integration:

- its async `Database` trait implemented on SurrealDB;
- a VRF key with its own lifecycle;
- epoch publishing.

In exchange it gives a verifiable map: non-membership and history proofs, and VRF-private
labels.

**Recommended and built:** Songbird's own RFC 9162 Merkle log (`crates/kt`, about 500 lines,
aws-lc-rs only, checkable by any CT library), behind the same endpoints, with `akd` recorded as
the upgrade path. The trade-off: a log, unlike a map, lets a server withhold an account's newest
entry from one reader. It cannot rewrite what it logged without a detectable fork. This is an
owner-level choice and is asked below.

## What was built

1. **`songbird-kt`**:
   - RFC 9162 leaf and node hashes, inclusion and consistency proofs and their verification;
   - signed tree heads;
   - leaves naming accounts by a salted HMAC;
   - a store trait that keeps only perfect-subtree hashes.
2. **Registry storage** (`crates/accounts/src/kt.rs`):
   - leaves and nodes in SurrealDB;
   - appends serialized on the head row;
   - every node a computation needs fetched in one round (a dry run lists them, since they
     depend only on indices);
   - a reconcile pass the worker runs, logging each identity key, device list and username
     change once, driven by a generation bumped in each change's transaction.
3. **`api`** (`services/api/src/kt.rs`, PROTOCOL.md §4.9): `GET /v1/kt/head` (signed at serving
   time by the log key), `POST /v1/kt/account` (salt, head, entries with proofs),
   `POST /v1/kt/consistency`.
4. **Security notifications**, documented as the three paths a device-list change takes:
   - the signed notice to the account's own devices;
   - the send path's stale-list refusal to contacts;
   - the log.
   Identity keys never change in place.
5. **GDPR**: deleting an account deletes its salt, so its entries stay in the append-only log but
   name no one.
6. Docs: PROTOCOL.md §4.9 and §13 (CT reference roots); threat model §1 (one row, awaiting
   approval), §3.15 and open risks; D-0055; `[api.kt]`; fuzz target `kt`, in the nightly matrix.

| `crates/kt` `every_proof_of_every_tree_up_to_70_leaves_verifies_and_tampering_fails` | Every inclusion proof (all indices of all sizes 1–70) and every consistency proof (all pairs) verifies. Another leaf, index or size, a changed proof hash, or a truncated consistency proof fails |

## Open risks

- A transparency log, not a map. A server can withhold the newest entry from one reader, so the
  log relies on owners checking their own entries and on clients gossiping heads.
- There are no non-membership proofs or VRF privacy: whoever has an account ID sees how many keys
  and lists it has had. `akd` is the upgrade path.
- The log key is not deployed yet: it goes in the secrets store, and apps pin it.
- There is no external auditor yet.
