## What M1 built

| Piece | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verify-only provider | `crates/groups/src/crypto.rs` | OpenMLS's crypto trait on aws-lc-rs: hashes, Ed25519 verification, HMAC, HKDF. Every operation that needs a secret (AEAD, HPKE, signing, key generation) is refused, so the server cannot be made to use one. |
| Delivery-service core | `crates/groups/src/ds.rs`, `state.rs`, `wire.rs` | OpenMLS `PublicGroup` plus Songbird's checks before any merge; state stored as OpenMLS's format in postcard, version-tagged; the commit bundle and `GroupInfo` parsers. |
| X-Wing | `crypto.rs`, `crates/keys` | Every group uses `0x004F` (ML-KEM-768 and X25519). KeyPackages for it are accepted on upload, both key halves checked. |
| Registry | `crates/accounts/src/groups.rs` | One record per group (epoch, state, roster, last commit hash). The epoch is compared and set together with the commit's fan-out, in one transaction. |
| `api` | `services/api/src/groups.rs` | `POST /v1/groups`, `/v1/groups/commit` and `/v1/groups/state` (PROTOCOL §6.2–6.4): answers after every mailbox stored the fan-out, uses fan-out keys only servers can derive, recognises a retried commit, re-drives after a crash, and spends a per-device commit budget. |
| Send path | `crates/send/src/groups.rs`, `crates/proto/src/mls.rs` | The `GROUP` flag `0x40` (PROTOCOL §6.5): a `PrivateMessage` checked against a cached roster and fanned out to it; reasons 12 and 13. Delivery never links OpenMLS. |
| Retirement | `crates/proto`, `crates/client`, `services/api` | The `Send` flag `0x01` (D-0026) is refused; the sealed endpoint refuses `GROUP`. |
| Client harness | `tests/groups` | Simulated devices on OpenMLS's own RustCrypto provider, as apps would run it. |

## The gate, item by item

The plan's M1 gate, as written, and what meets it.

| Gate item | Met by | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Provider known answers, and byte-for-byte interop with OpenMLS's own provider** | `crypto.rs`; the harness | RFC 5869 (HKDF) and RFC 8032 (Ed25519) known answers. Interop is shown by every harness and live test: the server's provider verifies what OpenMLS's RustCrypto provider makes, and its KeyPackage references equal OpenMLS's (`keypackages.rs`). *Changed from the plan:* `tools/openmls-vectors` was not needed, because the harness runs OpenMLS itself inside the tests. |
| **Concurrent-commit races never fork: N clients commit on one epoch, one wins, all converge (live, SurrealDB)** | `gate_p05`; `convergence.rs` | Live: five members commit over HTTP at once, round after round. Exactly one 200 per epoch, the rest 409 with the new epoch, and every client ends at the same epoch. 30 of 30 passes over ten runs. In-process: a six-way race. |
| **Property tests: honest clients end at the server's epoch and roster, over random interleavings** | `convergence.rs` | `honest_clients_always_agree_with_the_server`: 24 random sequences of adds, removals and races in random server orders. After every step, every client has the server's epoch and roster, and every member reads every other member's message. |
| **A crash between store and fan-out is re-driven exactly once** | `gate_p05` | `a_lost_answer_is_recognised_and_an_interrupted_fan_out_is_redriven`: a commit recorded without its fan-out is delivered by one re-drive pass and not by the second. A commit submitted twice is stored once and answered the same both times. |
| **Fuzz targets for every handshake decoder** | `mls_wire`, `mls_commit` | Two targets instead of the five the plan named:<br>• `mls_wire`: the `PrivateMessage` prefix, the bundle and the `GroupInfo` signer;<br>• `mls_commit`: arbitrary bundles through `apply_commit` against a real group (so OpenMLS's `PublicMessage`, Welcome and `GroupInfo` decoding), and arbitrary stored records.<br>60 s each, ASan: 8,477,711 and 254,179 runs, nothing found. Both added to the nightly CI matrix, where they were missing. *Limit:* `mls_commit` cannot yet reach inside a validly signed commit (follow-ups). |
| **`cargo tree` count recorded** | D-0066, D-0068 | 11 new crates in `api`'s build. Delivery, worker and push do not link OpenMLS (`cargo tree -i openmls`). |

### Every refusal, tested

`tests/groups/tests/lifecycle.rs` and `crates/send/src/tests.rs` refuse, each with its own test:

- a commit sent by another device than its signer;
- an unknown device added, or a device added twice;
- a missing, swapped or extra Welcome;
- a stale or foreign `GroupInfo`;
- a stale epoch (answered with the current one);
- the size and bundle limits;
- a commit from a forked group;
- an application message submitted as a commit;
- a stranger's external commit, and a second leaf for one device;
- the creation checks;
- group messages from non-members, to unknown groups, at old or future epochs, of another group,
  that are handshakes, or that are not MLS. Nothing refused is stored.

2. **Two threat-model §1 rows** (`docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`, marked "awaiting the owner's approval"):
   - per group: the epoch, the roster (member devices; their accounts follow from the device
     lists), OpenMLS's public state (each leaf's device ID, public keys and signatures), the
     current `GroupInfo` and the hash of the commit that made the epoch. No secret, name or
     message. Anyone who reads the registry can tell which devices share a group: the
     metadata D-0065 §2 accepted;
   - a commit's pending fan-out (the commit and Welcome as uploaded, and the recipient device
     IDs) until every mailbox stored it: milliseconds normally, seconds after a crash.
3. **The licence exceptions** (D-0068): three MPL-2.0 crates, used only by the test harness.

## Open risks (threat model §6)

- A device removed by a commit can still post at its last epoch for up to a second, on a
  delivery node whose roster cache has not refreshed. Members who processed the removal see who
  sent it.
- Groups are not yet in the GDPR export or deletion. They join with M2, since deleting an account
  must also remove its leaves.
- The mailboxes keep their now-unused epoch table (from the retired flag `0x01`) until their
  next storage format change.
